### Decision

No production optimization was made. The repeated measurements are not stable across all relevant cases: for example, 1,024-target/1-incoming overlapping merge changed from 81 µs to 308 µs, and one-layer disjoint composite load changed from 32 µs to 74 µs. Several larger cases are closer between runs, but this shared-host variation prevents treating a small candidate delta as reliable. The baseline therefore does not establish an abnormal merge cost, and no candidate has been shown to meet the required 10% improvement with no repeatable regression above 5%. The existing prevalidated `BTreeMap::append` commit path and its transactional behavior remain unchanged. A controlled candidate comparison would be required before optimizing.
