## Per-case timing and RSS summary

A local accounting check showed that a parent waiting for one 300 MiB child reports about 300 MiB, while two concurrent 150 MiB children report about 150 MiB: this is the peak of an individual process in the waited process tree, not the simultaneous sum across processes. It is not a per-function allocation metric. The raw `/usr/bin/time` report also contains the child’s own `load_elapsed_ns`; elapsed times include process launch and teardown. RSS granularity and operating-system accounting limit interpretation; use same-host before/after comparisons rather than attributing the total to `SourceLoadSession::child` alone.
